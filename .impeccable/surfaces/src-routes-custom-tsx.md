# /custom — التفصيل حسب الطلب

Mode: Persuade. Audience: shoppers in Saudi Arabia, mostly on phones, wanting a piece built to their size and taste. Action: submit a custom request (recorded with a TFS- number, then WhatsApp). Proof: real categories, the fabrics already offered on made-to-order catalogue pieces, the admin-managed story images, and admin-managed completed projects (the section is hidden when there are none). Constraints: no invented prices, timelines, deposits, delivery promises or warranties.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a commission order sheet, not a marketing landing. It refuses the icon-card grid and stat strip and leads with the line «أنت تختار الفكرة، ونحن نهتم بالتفاصيل.»

OWN-WORLD: The incumbent TARAF world. Silk/ink fields with gold hairlines alternate with cream/ivory bands. Display face Cormorant/Amiri, body face El Messiri, 2px corners. Gold is used only for actions and rules.

STORY: The visitor learns they bring the idea and TARAF handles the detail. They see real categories, the process, what they can specify, the craft and real projects, then fill in the sheet and get a request number.

FIRST VIEWPORT: lg: two columns over silk. The headline is display scale on the start side with a lead, a gold «ابدأ طلب التفصيل» button and a WhatsApp ghost button. On the end side is a tall framed brand photo crossed by a gold measuring line. Mobile: the photo band sits on top and the headline and CTA sit below, within the first viewport.

FORM: Extension of the incumbent world, shaped directly (precise brief). seed: none (unavailable)
Signature interaction: a live elevation drawing. Typing width, depth and height redraws a proportional outline with dimension lines.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
